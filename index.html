About Us

Our Heritage

Founded in 1998, Teakwood began as a small family workshop dedicated to preserving the art of traditional woodworking. Today, we bridge the gap between classic craftsmanship and modern minimalist design.

We source all our wood sustainably, ensuring that every piece of furniture not only brings beauty to your home but also protects our planet's forests for generations to come. Quality, sustainability, and timeless design are at the core of everything we do.
